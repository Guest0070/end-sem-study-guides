U1-4515-mark case · numerical

Case (15 marks). A mid-sized Indian textile exporter is asked by its European buyer for its carbon footprint and ESG disclosures. It burns 5,00,000 litres of diesel (EF 2.68 kgCO₂/L), buys 8,000 MWh of grid power (0.72 tCO₂/MWh) and its inbound logistics emit 1,200 t. Revenue ₹600 crore.

(a) Compute Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions and intensity (5). (b) Identify material E, S and G issues (5). (c) Recommend a disclosure and risk-management plan (5).

Answer

(a) Scope 1 = 1,340 t; Scope 2 = 5,760 t; Scope 3 = 1,200 t; total 8,300 tCO₂e; intensity 13.83 t per ₹ crore.

(b) E: energy, water use and effluents from dyeing, chemicals. S: labour conditions, wages, worker safety in the supply chain. G: board oversight, transparency, anti-bribery.

(c) Adopt GHG Protocol accounting and a BRSR-style report with GRI index; set intensity targets; switch to rooftop solar/renewable PPAs (cuts Scope 2); supplier code of conduct and audits; ESG committee at board level; prepare for EU CBAM-style and supply-chain due-diligence requirements; seek an ESG rating or buyer certification.
