U1-202 marks

Distinguish between risk and uncertainty.

Answer

Under risk, possible outcomes and their probabilities can be estimated (measurable variability). Under uncertainty, probabilities cannot be assigned. Capital budgeting risk techniques (ENPV, σ, CE, RADR) work on risk.

U1-212 marks

What is a certainty equivalent coefficient?

Answer

αt = certain cash flow ÷ risky (expected) cash flow for year t, between 0 and 1. Risky flows are multiplied by α and discounted at the risk-free rate.

U1-222 marks

How is the risk-adjusted discount rate determined?

Answer

RADR = risk-free rate + risk premium. The premium can be set by management for the risk class of the project or estimated with CAPM: Rf + β(Rm − Rf).

What is break-even (margin of safety) in sensitivity analysis?

Answer

The percentage change in a variable (cost, cash flow, discount rate, life) that reduces NPV to zero. A small percentage means the project is highly sensitive to that variable.

List the steps in Monte Carlo simulation.

Answer

(1) Build the NPV model; (2) specify probability distributions for key variables; (3) assign random-number ranges (cumulative probabilities); (4) draw random numbers and compute NPV; (5) repeat many times; (6) analyse the distribution (mean, σ, P(NPV < 0)).

Answer

Section A · Q25 marks

What is value-based management? Explain how a company like HUL can use EVA to implement it.

Answer key

VBM is a management approach that aligns strategy, capital allocation, performance measurement and pay with shareholder value creation.

  1. Adopt EVA (NOPAT − capital × WACC) as the central measure.
  2. Break EVA into drivers for each brand/unit: margin, asset turnover, working capital.
  3. Approve only investments that earn above WACC.
  4. Set EVA-improvement targets and link bonuses to them.
  5. Release capital from low-return assets; keep negative working capital (HUL's model).

Limitations: accounting adjustments and short-term bias.
